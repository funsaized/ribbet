# Explanation

These pages explain the choices behind Ribbit and its examples. They are useful when deciding how to design a workflow or interpret its results.

- [When to chain models](model-chaining.md): where a small intermediate step can help, and how to compare it fairly.
- [Why the examples retain originals](recipes.md): a walkthrough of the triage, context, and brief designs.
- [How records and pipes compose](composition.md): data boundaries, identity, metadata, and flow budgets.
- [Execution and trust](trust.md): what models, built-ins, extensions, and external harnesses can do.
- [Model evidence](../models.md): measured results and known failure modes.
- [Product direction](../product-direction.md): the intended users, scope, and product requirements.

For commands you can follow now, use the [how-to guides](../how-to/index.md).
